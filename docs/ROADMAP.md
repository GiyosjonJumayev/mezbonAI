# Roadmap: features that bring travellers and income

Ordered by what they are worth against what they cost to build. ✅ means built.

## Bring travellers

| # | Feature | Why it matters | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Google and share previews**: each offer page carries its title, description, picture, price and rating for search engines and for links shared in Telegram, WhatsApp and Instagram; a sitemap lists every offer in ten languages | Free traffic from search, and shared links that look worth opening | ✅ 10.0 |
| 2 | **Promo codes** for hotels, guides, bloggers and campaigns, paid from the platform's share | Measurable marketing; the maker's income does not change | ✅ 10.0 |
| 3 | Resellers through the OCTO standard | Tour operators, hotels and apps sell the offers | ✅ 9.0 |
| 4 | Booking box for the maker's own website | Makers bring their own guests | ✅ 9.0 |
| 5 | **Theme pages**: crafts, food, village life, Fergana Valley, two hours or less | Better landing pages for search and ads | ✅ 11.0 |
| 6 | **Gift vouchers** | Diaspora and friends buy a visit as a present; money arrives early | ✅ 12.0 |
| 7 | **Give 10%, get 10%** for guests who share | Word of mouth with a reason | ✅ 11.0 |
| 8 | **Guest booking updates in Telegram** | The messenger people here already use | ✅ 12.0 |

## Turn visitors into bookings

| # | Feature | Why it matters | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Win back unfinished bookings**: one e-mail when a held place was not paid and is still free | Recovers sales that were nearly lost | ✅ 10.0 |
| 10 | **Instant confirmation** for makers who want it | No waiting for an answer; more guests finish | ✅ 10.0 |
| 11 | Live places, next free time, waiting list | Urgency and no dead ends | ✅ 8.0 |
| 12 | **Group requests** with a quote from the maker | Schools, companies, cruise and rail groups | ✅ 11.0 |

## Earn from makers and partners

| # | Feature | Why it matters | State |
|---|---|---|---|

## After this list

- Certification with GetYourGuide and Viator through the reseller API.
- Real photos and short videos from every maker.
